Hello everyone!

I'm Froso, a product designer from sunny Cyprus, currently navigating my third year of International Experience Design at Thomas More University in Belgium.

I love turning ideas into experiences that actually make sense (and look good while doing it). From the first “all-over-the-place” brainstorm to the final prototype, I've worked on designing digital products from the ground up. Think user research, interviews, user flows, wireframes, branding, UI design, and even bringing designs to life through code.

I'm all about creating experiences that are not only functional but also fun, meaningful, and a little unexpected.
